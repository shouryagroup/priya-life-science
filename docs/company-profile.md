# COMPANY PROFILE

## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

**Priya Life Science** is a partnership firm engaged in the manufacturing, marketing, and supply of pharmaceutical and nutraceutical products.

The firm was established in **2008** with the vision of providing quality pharmaceutical products and reliable manufacturing services.

### Partners

1. **Mr. Hasmukh Chimanbhai Patel**
2. **Mr. Trushar Hasmukhbhai Patel**

The partners are pharmacy graduates with extensive experience in various areas of the pharmaceutical industry, including manufacturing, product development, and marketing.

With their professional expertise and industry knowledge, the firm has focused its activities on the pharmaceutical and nutraceutical sectors.

Priya Life Science established its own manufacturing facility during **2008–09**, with the objective of developing and manufacturing quality pharmaceutical formulations.

The company operates through two major business segments:

- **Own Brand Products**
- **Third-Party / Contract Manufacturing**

Our products are marketed across various parts of India. The company is also engaged in merchant exports to **Afghanistan, Europe, Africa, and other international markets** through its business associates and export partners.

With more than a decade of experience and a strong commitment to quality, Priya Life Science has established itself as a growing pharmaceutical company in Gujarat.

The company has maintained a strong growth trajectory and has developed a reputation for:

- Quality products
- Dependable services
- Long-term business relationships

## 2. MANUFACTURING FACILITIES

Priya Life Science operates a well-equipped manufacturing facility designed to support the production of a wide range of pharmaceutical formulations.

### Key Manufacturing Facilities

- Modern and adequately equipped manufacturing infrastructure.
- Dedicated **Liquid Oral Manufacturing Section** for the production of:
  - Syrups
  - Suspensions
  - Drops
  - Other liquid formulations
- **Capsule Manufacturing and Packaging Sections** with appropriate arrangements for manufacturing different categories of capsules.
- Separate arrangements for the manufacturing of **antibiotic and vitamin tablets and capsules**, wherever applicable, to minimize the risk of cross-contamination.
- Systematic manufacturing facilities designed to support efficient production operations and consistent product quality.
- Adequate infrastructure for handling different pharmaceutical formulations in accordance with applicable manufacturing requirements.

## 3. QUALITY CONTROL

Quality is one of the core principles of Priya Life Science.
